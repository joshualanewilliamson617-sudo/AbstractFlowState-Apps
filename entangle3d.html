How mass changes hands

Two bodies meeting slowly merge — the heavier keeps its identity and takes the lighter one's mass. Meeting fast, both shatter and the debris scatters to whoever is closer. Gas slows everything down, so a gas-rich disk wins exchanges it would otherwise lose.

When one side holds 68% of all mass, the scales tip and its core ignites.
