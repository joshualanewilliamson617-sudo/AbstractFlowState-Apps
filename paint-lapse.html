    Start a fresh file every

    Each finished piece is a complete video on disk, so a crash can only ever cost you the piece in progress.

    1. Open Paint.
    2. Press Start recording and choose a folder for the video — a local one such as C:\Videos, never a folder inside OneDrive.
    3. Press it again and pick the Paint window.
    4. Paint. This tab can sit in the background or be minimized.
    5. Come back and press Stop and make MP4.

    If a recording stops on its own
    • Don't save into OneDrive. It locks video files while they're being written, and later refuses to open them with error 0x80070323. Use a plain local folder such as C:\Videos. Watch out for Desktop and Documents, which Windows often redirects into OneDrive.
    • To catch game sound, set the speed to Real time, share Entire screen, and tick the share-audio box in the picker. A single-window share can't carry sound on Windows.
    • Open the Session log while recording. It says outright whether the keep-awake tone is running and whether frames are still arriving.
    • In Edge, open Settings, then System and performance, and turn off sleeping tabs. Chrome's version is called Memory Saver.
    • Keep the laptop plugged in, and set Windows to never sleep while plugged in.
    • Leave the silent tone on. A tab making sound is treated as in use.
    • Check there's free space on the drive you're saving to. A full drive ends the recording.
    • Whatever happened, every finished piece is already saved. Open the Session log to see where it stopped.
