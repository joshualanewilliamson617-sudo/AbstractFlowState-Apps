Shell Lock

Your amber line grows from the center of the bubble. Move line swings it around the sphere. Expand and Contract stretch it in and out from the center. Rotate bubble spins the whole world 360°. Put the line's tip on the cyan dot and hold it there until the ring fills.

Keyboard: WASD rotate · arrows move line · E expand · Q contract · Esc menu
