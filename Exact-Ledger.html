Exact Ledger

Every answer is kept as an exact fraction, so any digit can be found, even at position 101000. Results are saved to your history and notes.

Expression
/ divides the opposite direction: 10 / 2 means 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2. Use ÷ for normal division.
[0.+] at the front moves the decimal point right: [00.+]5 = 500. ×0 at the end moves it left: 5×00 = 0.05. One zero per place, and only one of the two per expression.
· is a multiplication dot and works the same as ×.
% splits a number into two even halves: 10% = 5 | 5, and the result carries 5 forward.
Type on your keyboard too. Enter calculates. Use Ans for the last answer or H3 for history item 3.
