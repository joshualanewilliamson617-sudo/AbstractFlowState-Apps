DEAD BLOCK

An endless neighborhood after dark. The dead drift out of the fog from every direction. Roam the streets, raid supply caches, and clear block after block. Each sector they come back tougher, faster and in bigger numbers.

HOW TO PLAY
W A S DMove · hold Shift to run SPACE · Q · C · VJump · Slide · Crouch · Dive. Rebind any key in CONTROLS. MOUSEClick to lock aim, look around, click to fire 1 – 7 / WHEELKnife · Machete · Chainsaw · Pistol · Shotgun · Rifle · Heavy MG F · ESCFlashlight · Pause WHEEL CLICKZoom in / out (rebindable) MOVEBottom-left stick. Push all the way to run. Double-tap it to lock auto-run forward; steer with the aim stick or slide the move stick sideways. Pull back or double-tap again to stop. AIM STICKRight thumb. Push further to turn faster. Swiping anywhere else on the right also looks. FIREHold the green radar button to fire, and slide your thumb on it to fine-aim. ZOOMTap the AIM stick to zoom in, tap again to zoom out. ACTION PAD3 buttons above the aim stick. Pick which actions they hold (Jump, Slide, Dive, Crouch) in CONTROLS. Push the move stick all the way to run. WEAPONSTap the rail at the bottom. CONTROLLERLeft stick move · right stick aim · RT fire · A jump · B slide · X dive · Y crouch · L3 run · LB/RB weapons · Start pause COMBOSRUN + JUMP = Leap · RUN + SLIDE = Power Slide (bowls zombies over) · SLIDE in mid-air = Ground Pound shockwave. Jump onto car hoods, then roofs, to get above the horde. TELEPORTERSGlowing pads with light beams stand on the four corners of every district. Step on one to warp to another corner gate or a random drop somewhere across the city. CACHESWalk over glowing crates for ammo, fuel and med kits. WEATHER & WRECKSLightning can strike a tree and drop it across the street. It crushes anything under it, you included. Shoot a car a few times and it blows up, taking nearby zombies (and you) with it, and it can set off other cars. CROUCHStay low to sneak. While you're crouched and not shooting, zombies more than 9m away lose track of you and wander. DIVEThrow yourself forward, untouchable until you land. Lands you on the ground for a moment.
