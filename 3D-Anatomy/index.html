    How to make a pack

    A pack is a small .js file. It can add a tab, tours and body conditions. Only add packs you trust: a pack runs as part of this page.

    AnatomyPacks.register({
  id: 'conditions', name: 'Body conditions',
  conditions: [{ name: 'Osteoporosis', sys: 'skel',
    group: 'Spine', text: 'Bones lose density...' }],
  lessons: [{ name: 'My tour', steps: [
    { sys: 'card', title: 'Heart', text: '...',
      match: 'Heart', sec: 'Part 1' }] }],
  tab: { title: 'My tab',
    build(el, api) { el.innerHTML = '...'; } }
});
    How to use this app
    About 3D Anatomy

    Eleven organ systems on scan-derived male and female bodies, about 3,100 named structures, with a live heartbeat, breathing, lessons and quizzes. Drag the body to turn it; tap or click any structure.

    On a phone, one finger scrolls the page; two fingers on the body turn it (slide) and zoom (pinch); tap a part for its card. Double-tap the body for spin mode, where one finger spins it; double-tap again to scroll. With a mouse, drag to turn; hold Ctrl and turn the wheel (or press + and −) to zoom; the wheel on its own scrolls the page.

    Keys: ← → change system, + − zoom, space plays the systems tour, Esc closes menus and clears the selection. Educational reference, not for diagnosis.
