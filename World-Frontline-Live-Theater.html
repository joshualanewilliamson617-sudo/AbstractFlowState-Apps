CONTROLS & HELP

Commander (live map): Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, click a sector to select it. Choose a unit and hit Auto-deploy. Units travel across the real map and fight on arrival. Red and Blue keep fighting each other — and you — the whole time.

First person: Pick a sector, then press one of the 5 “Lead it yourself” buttons. Click the screen to capture the mouse (Esc releases). Press M for the live world map, which keeps updating during the mission.

🪖 Ground: WASD move • mouse look • Click shoot • Right-click aim • R reload • Shift sprint • Space jump. Stand on the flag to capture it — enemies nearby pause the capture.

🛡️ Tank: W/S drive • A/D turn hull • mouse aims turret • Click / Space main cannon • Right-click / F coax MG • Z zoom sight.

🚁 Chopper: WASD fly • Space/E climb • C/Q descend • mouse look • Click 30mm gun • Right-click / G Hellfire (locks the target nearest your crosshair).

🛩️ Jet: Mouse or W/S pitch • A/D roll • Q/E rudder • Shift/X throttle up • Z throttle down • Click cannon • Right-click / Space missile (auto-lock).

✈️ Gunship: Mouse aims the sensor while you orbit • Click fire • 1/2/3 or Q/E switch 25mm / 40mm / 105mm • wheel or Z zoom • T toggles white-hot / black-hot.

🔧 Repair & recuperate: Every mission has a green repair point for you — tank depot, chopper helipad (land on it), jet resupply gate (fly through it), ground aid station, and in-flight repair for the gunship (H, 45s cooldown). Each enemy map also has 2 orange enemy depots: damaged enemies retreat there to repair, and each depot sends reinforcements every ~30s. Destroy the depots to stop both.

Touch: Left stick moves, drag the right side to look, FIRE / ALT buttons, ▲▼ change altitude (chopper).
